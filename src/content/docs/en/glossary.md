The glossary will grow with the published chapters. Each term receives a stable bilingual mapping, and overloaded terms are qualified by their model, training, runtime, or system layer.

| Term | Meaning | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Training | Estimating or optimizing model parameters from training data, including counts or learned weights. | Not inference. |
| Inference | Model computation performed with fixed parameters. | Not a synonym for reasoning. |
| Reasoning | Intermediate computation or search organized to solve a problem. | Not a synonym for inference. |
| Context | Information visible to the current request. | Not parameters, long-term memory, or agent state. |
