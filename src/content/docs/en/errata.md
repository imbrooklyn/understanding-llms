If you find a factual, technical, translation, code, link, or accessibility problem, open an [Erratum Issue](https://github.com/imbrooklyn/understanding-llms/issues/new?template=erratum.yml).

Include the page URL, location, current content, and proposed correction. Confirmed errata will be recorded in the changelog.
