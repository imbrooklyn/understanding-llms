## Audit and editorial direction

The designated master-plan sections, contribution rules, implementation plan, configuration, eight chapter files, and CH-05 boundary have been reviewed. Existing heading numbering, KaTeX, numerical functions, and draft filtering are retained. Earlier drafts overused reader prompts and browser instructions; complete evidence was scattered among controls and collapsed answers.

The structural reference is OSTEP's preface and CPU Scheduling chapter: define the problem, assumptions, and criterion; establish a baseline with a complete example; use a counterexample to motivate a mechanism; then explain its tradeoff. Adopt that teaching structure without copying prose, diagrams, or distinctive expressions. Figures must participate in an argument, and exercises must transfer the method to new cases. Word counts and passing software tests do not establish pedagogical quality.

The Chinese edition follows natural Mainland Chinese textbook usage. Establish the source meaning before rewriting, then choose Chinese sentence structure independently of the English syntax. Preserve facts, logic, qualifications, terminology, and numerical evidence while allowing sentence splitting, combination, and reordering. Review paragraphs, headings, table labels, figure descriptions, exercises, solutions, and references together; a fluent paragraph cannot compensate for opaque labels elsewhere.

## Chapter contracts

The accessibility revision assumes familiarity with everyday chat and basic arithmetic, without programming, linear algebra, or calculus. It fills prerequisite gaps within the argument instead of assigning preparatory reading. Chapter 1 introduces the assistant/model/application distinction and probabilities through replaceable tickets. Chapter 2 carries input, stored values, and results through a multiplication example, then explains encoding, lookup, target alignment, and parameter updates. Chapter 3 moves from reusable pieces to exact lookup, then from coordinates to arrows, lengths, and the cosine ratio. Chapter 4 motivates its model names after the Unigram failure, derives conditional probabilities from row-specific ticket bags, expands general notation back into the `ab` example, and keeps exact pseudorandom arithmetic after the generation trace. Both languages receive equivalent explanatory additions. The fixed data, formulas, six figures, and 35 transfer exercises remain intact.

## Fixed data and evidence rules

- All transcripts, policies, orders, vectors, corpora, and illustrative distributions are original constructions, identified as such. Data is CC BY-SA 4.0; code is Apache-2.0. Never describe authored cases as measured model accuracy.
- Keep G1–G5 inputs, outputs, configurations, and evidence intact. The fictional Beijing limit changes from 600 to 750; A-104 is processing. A status query performs zero mutations; the later cancellation example requires the stipulated approval.
- CH-02's A/B parameter tables are completely assigned functions. They do not derive probabilities from the toy embeddings or demonstrate neural learning. Show every scenario without a selector.
- CH-03 uses exact longest-prefix matching, case sensitivity, no normalization, no inserted boundary tokens, and a one-code-point unknown fallback to reserved ID 0. The full table and version change are visible. Hand-assigned vectors support lookup and geometry, not empirical semantic claims.
- CH-04 trains on ab/ab/ac with predictable vocabulary a,b,c,EOS. BOS is context-only, and records never cross sequence boundaries. The ac/aa collection is a diagnostic fixture, not an independent generalization benchmark. Preserve the distinction between zero probability and undefined normalization. State candidate order, LCG32, seed 42, and the 12-draw limit including EOS.
- The primary-source ledger documents technical support. OSTEP supports editorial decisions only. New exercise results are derived independently and checked against the implementation.

## Static infrastructure and review

Body headings begin at `##`; compilation adds section numbers and stable bilingual anchors. Worked examples and tables have chapter-local numbers. Figures have captions, descriptions, references in the prose, and adjacent licenses. KaTeX emits local HTML and MathML.

Reader-facing chapter labels use localized `Chapter 1` wording; page openings separate this label from the title, and sidebar entries use compact numeric labels. The roadmap and prose references follow the same convention. Internal `ch-01` routes and identifiers remain unchanged. Chapter 1 uses descriptive case names and first/second record references, while the original evaluation, failure, and risk IDs remain in fixtures. Cited works appear under `References` with explanations tied to the chapter argument.

Learning content contains no forms, buttons, inputs, selectors, custom interactive elements, or collapsed answers. All alternatives, calculations, and solutions are visible. Site navigation, language selection, and search remain available. Numerical code serves author verification rather than a required reader interaction.
