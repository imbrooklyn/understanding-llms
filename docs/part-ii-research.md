## Decisions and independent derivations

- The chapter examples use explicit axes and units. Matmul contracts coordinates, not text positions; changing a label mapping can leave shape unchanged while changing meaning.
- The softmax example begins with weights 2,1,1; logarithms yield the supplied logits. Target losses, their mean and perplexity follow from fractions 1/2 and 1/4. Shift invariance follows by cancelling the shared exponential factor. KL uses a separate two-outcome example and checks both directions and support failure.
- The paper training step is independently derived from the three local sensitivities of score, probability and negative log loss. Finite differences and PyTorch later verify it; neither generates its own expected derivative.
- The classifier split is frozen before fitting. Test values are reported only for the validation-selected snapshot. Long-run training/validation curves diagnose overfitting; there is no test curve used for model selection.
- Search uses a frozen document corpus with old and current rules both retained. All methods share raw text, processing, candidate order, relevance labels and tie/abstention rules. Lexical relevance cannot enforce effective policy dates. Report every retained failure.
- A hand-authored dense map is explicitly an assumption-driven diagnostic, not trained Word2Vec. The actual NNLM learns its own rows from declared language-training data; retrieving with those rows is a separate measured task, with no promise of improvement.
- The normalizer is a pure string-to-string function with type checks. Canonical composition, case folding and whitespace collapse are separately motivated choices, not a universal cleaning rule. Negation, digits and punctuation remain; raw source is retained outside the key.
