## Context and output contracts

- Liu et al., [Lost in the Middle](https://aclanthology.org/2024.tacl-1.9.pdf), TACL 2024, pp. 157–173, §§2–3 and §5. Controlled document-position/length changes in multi-document QA and key/value retrieval motivate separate presence and utilization tests. These experiments concern the studied models/tasks; they do not establish an immutable position curve for all future models. Our budget tests measure serialization and evidence retention, not LLM attention or accuracy.
- [RFC 8259](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8259.html), December 2017, §§2–7, 8.1, 9: JSON values, object members, arrays, number/string distinction, UTF-8 and parser limits. Duplicate names have interoperability hazards; this application's duplicate rejection is a stricter explicit policy.
- [JSON Schema Validation 2020-12](https://json-schema.org/draft/2020-12/json-schema-validation), §§6.1.1–6.1.3, 6.2, 6.4, 6.5.3: types, enum/const, numeric/array constraints and required properties. [Core](https://json-schema.org/draft/2020-12/json-schema-core), §§10.2.1–10.2.2 and 10.3.2: logical/conditional composition, properties and additionalProperties. Required is not non-null; structural acceptance does not establish truth, access rights or execution. Local validator uses the full declared dialect; vendor grammar subsets are a different contract.
- Anthropic, [Structured outputs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/structured-outputs), verified live on the date above: “How it works”, “How SDK transformation works”, “JSON Schema limitations”, “Invalid outputs”. Current raw request field is `output_config.format`; `strict: true` applies to tool definitions. SDK convenience arguments and old beta compatibility differ. Refusal and truncation can bypass ordinary output shape; unsupported numerical constraints can require local validation. Documentation also states an enum-capitalization caveat: we retain exact local enum validation, not silent permissive coercion. No commercial call, latency, price or model-quality result is claimed here.
- Anthropic, [Tool use overview](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/agents-and-tools/tool-use/overview), client tools versus server tools and tool-use lifecycle. Model output requests use; an application or provider service performs execution. The book implements only application-owned local reads.

## HTTP and protocol boundaries

- [RFC 9110](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110.html), June 2022, §§3.3, 4.2, 6, 9.2, 10.2.3, 11 and 15: client/server roles, URI, request/response, safe/idempotent methods, Retry-After, authentication and statuses. “Safe” concerns requested semantics, not absence of logging; idempotent intended effects do not require identical responses. A timeout alone does not prove non-execution.
- [RFC 6585](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6585.html), April 2012, §4: 429 and optional Retry-After; no universal rate policy implied. [WHATWG SSE](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/server-sent-events.html), living standard, §§9.2.4–9.2.6: UTF-8 event-stream framing, blank-line dispatch and reconnection. Application fragments need not be complete JSON values or model tokens.
- MCP official [2026-07-28 specification](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28), [base protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28/basic), “Statelessness”, “Messages”, “General fields”; [stdio](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28/basic/transports/stdio), “Request Metadata”, “Shutdown”; [discovery](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28/server/discover), request/result and “When to Call”; [tools](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28/server/tools), “Listing/Calling Tools”, “Structured Content”, “Output Schema” and error handling. The executable teaching adapter pins this revision, includes required metadata on every request and `resultType` on complete results, and offers discovery plus one read tool over actual subprocess stdio. It makes no full SDK/conformance claim. Self-reported clientInfo is never the business principal.
- MCP [resources](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28/server/resources) and [authorization](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28/basic/authorization): URI-addressed content and HTTP authorization framework occupy different layers. Local stdio does not perform remote OAuth. Discovery and readOnlyHint are not business authorization or evidence guarantees.
- MCP [changelog](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28/changelog), [deprecation registry](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28/deprecated), verified against [release announcement](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28/). Do not copy a 2025 initialize/initialized/session sequence into this new adapter. Roots, Sampling, Logging and Dynamic Client Registration are deprecated, not immediately removed. Their earliest removal is a revision on/after 2027-07-28; the older HTTP+SSE transport has its own registry schedule. Streamable HTTP can still use SSE. The snapshot overview contains leftover initialization wording; the detailed per-request rules and schema take precedence for our implementation. Legacy compatibility is a separately tested adapter, not an automatic downgrade of this example.

## Retrieval and grounded answers

- Robertson and Zaragoza, [The Probabilistic Relevance Framework: BM25 and Beyond](https://www.staff.city.ac.uk/~sbrp622/papers/foundations_bm25_review.pdf), 2009, §§3.4.2, 3.4.4–3.4.5, 3.5 (printed pp. 356–362): term-frequency saturation and soft length normalization, equations 3.10–3.15. The book explicitly chooses a positive `log(1 + ratio)` IDF variant and `(k1+1)` scaling; it does not mislabel these as the only original BM25 formula. [Trotman et al.](https://www.cs.otago.ac.nz/homepages/andrew/papers/2014-2.pdf), 2014, §2, documents implementation variants. Our tiny cards are pedagogical, not an IR benchmark.
- Karpukhin et al., [Dense Passage Retrieval](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.550.pdf), EMNLP 2020, §§2, 3.1–3.2: separate passage/query encoders, dot-product indexing and supervised contrastive training. Top-k answer-hit accuracy in the paper differs from multi-relevant-document Recall@k. Our reused NNLM mean vectors are a measured local representation baseline, not a reproduction of DPR; the separate three-axis map is authored, not trained.
- Cormack, Clarke and Buettcher, [Reciprocal Rank Fusion](https://cormack.uwaterloo.ca/cormacksigir09-rrf.pdf), SIGIR 2009, p. 758 formula and pilot choice `k=60`. The chapter writes the offset as the literal 60 to distinguish it from retrieval depth; missing documents contribute zero in our truncated-list variant. This is not score calibration or a proof that fusion must win.
- Nogueira and Cho, [Passage Re-ranking with BERT](https://arxiv.org/html/1901.04085v4), v4, 2020, §2 and §3.1: joint query/passage scoring after candidate retrieval; it cannot promote an absent candidate. Our executable scope reranker is a disclosed rule, not BERT or an LLM judge. No paper benchmark numbers are reused as local performance.
- Manning, Raghavan and Schütze, [Introduction to Information Retrieval, §8.4](https://nlp.stanford.edu/IR-book/html/htmledition/evaluation-of-ranked-retrieval-results-1.html): ranked evaluation, graded judgments and normalized discounted gain. Our binary Recall@k, reciprocal rank, exponential-gain nDCG and empty-gold convention are fully specified; macro denominators exclude empty-gold queries and report abstention separately.
- Lewis et al., [Retrieval-Augmented Generation](https://arxiv.org/html/2005.11401v4), NeurIPS 2020 / arXiv v4 2021, §§2.1–2.4, 3, 4.5: latent-document marginalization, DPR/BART, generator and query-encoder fine-tuning, frozen document encoder/index, and index replacement. The original architecture is not identical to a modern parse/chunk/pack/cite pipeline. Runtime evidence replacement in our system changes no model parameters; RAG research can still include training.
- Gao et al., [Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398.pdf), EMNLP 2023 (ALCE), §§2–3: statement-level citation support and separate correctness/citation evaluation. Our manual structured claim/evidence checker is narrower than their NLI judge and is not an ALCE score. Citation existence, support and answer coverage are separate tests.
