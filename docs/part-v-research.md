## Evidence classification and source handling

The model-card comparison and resource scenarios are author-assigned teaching inputs. The pipeline is original data processed by declared rules. Scalar/byte/timestamp outputs are derived, checked independently before implementation. MG-2 training results cited in Chapter 22 are existing local measurements with their original date and limitations; the new KA-1 runs have their own provenance. No third-party figures, tables, web corpus or model weights are copied into the book's assets. Diagrams are original explanatory arrangements.

Current model/service/software documentation and the remaining architecture, intervention, generation, serving and evaluation sources are recorded below after verification. An unverified external candidate cannot enter the measured comparison.

## Source-attribution correction found during editorial review

The frozen KA-1 adaptation metadata described every compact prompt as a reduction of KA-0. Re-reading D showed that it defines processing as a possible status, rather than assigning a current status. KA-1 assigns processing to a new fictional short identifier A104; no-granted-approval and unknown-passport conditions also extend the source meanings. `ka1-provenance-note-v1.json` records this correction after execution without changing the frozen prompts, rubric, results or decision. Both Chapter 32 pages and the engineering report now make this distinction explicit. It is a metadata attribution correction, not evidence of a live order lookup.
